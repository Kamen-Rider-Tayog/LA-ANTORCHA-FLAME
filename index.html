The booth is a warm and inviting destination, strategically located on the main school ground of Cavite State University-General Trias City Campus. Its design is a tribute to tradition, being directly inspired by the open and welcoming bahay-kubo (traditional Filipino home).


The booth's color palette of earthy brown and beige creates a calm, nostalgic environment reminiscent of a home softly lit by candlelight. This design delivers the warmth and creativity of Filipino craftsmanship.


The stall is visible and accessible to everyone—not just university students, but also their friends and acquaintances. It is open for you to visit every Monday to Friday, from 10:00 a.m.. to 5:00 p.m.. Come by to view the scented candle items, smell the products, and instantly connect with the brand's patriotic story.
